likely produce a NO-EDGE verdict. **That outcome is expected and

## 4. Drawdown budget (CHOSEN AT SIGN-OFF: **−64R**)

Derived only from pre-cutoff data (research + burned OOS, n=322 trades;
streams regenerated by the frozen pipeline, dry-run verified). Kill
semantics: cumulative forward R <= budget at any point -> KILL (the
peak-relative variant is reported for reference; it is stricter and is NOT
the kill rule). At f = 0.01 per R, a budget of −B R is −B% of initial
equity; a single stop costs 2R x 0.01 = 2%.

12-month horizon = 108 trades; moving-block bootstrap (block 10, 10,000
resamples), measured-edge and demeaned (zero-edge) variants:

| budget R | capital at risk | P(hit, measured) | P(hit, zero edge) | P(hit, peak-rel) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| −40 | 40% | 0.0373 | 0.2755 | 0.1245 |
| −50 | 50% | 0.0152 | 0.1698 | 0.0454 |
| **−64 (CHOSEN)** | 64% | 0.0038 | 0.0799 | 0.0088 |

Anchors: research-period peak-DD was −32.0R; pre-cutoff cumulative minimum
was −15.2572R (kill semantics — the known path never breached −16R
cumulative); longest time-under-water 287.2 days (nearly a full 12-month
window can be under water even with the measured edge). A budget that would
have killed the known path is incoherent with the premise; −40R carries the
thinnest safety margin over the known −32R peak-DD.
